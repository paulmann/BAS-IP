BAS-IP

Who paid, who may enter, who opened the gate

Gate barrier management for a residential complex: residents, monthly billing with recalculations, guest QR passes, per-open audit log, mailings and REST API. PHP 8.2+, no CDN.

What it does

  • Resident base with cars, phones and access flags
  • Monthly billing with recalculations and guest passes
  • Every gate opening written to the audit log

Built with

CSS, JavaScript, PHP
